# Team Lock Board
{: .no_toc }

**Studio feature.** The Team Lock Board is the Studio form of the [Scene Locks]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/scene-locks/) section.

## What the board shows

Every scene and prefab declaration across your team's local branches:

- **who** holds the declaration,
- on **which branch**,
- **since when**,
- whether the asset has **since changed on your branch**.

**Stale** and **duplicate** declarations are flagged, so the board stays trustworthy instead of becoming a wall of forgotten claims.

## The same honesty rule

The board is built from declarations that travel through your team's own commits, and the Standard limit applies unchanged: declarations are advisory warnings. The board shows who is editing what across branches — it cannot stop anyone from editing, and it does not pretend to. See [What v1 Is Not]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/what-v1-is-not/).
